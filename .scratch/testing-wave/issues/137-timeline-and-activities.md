A 6:30 PM soup then sits under 6:24 PM, above meals eaten at 6:25 and 6:26. Chain the 30-minute window from the card's last meal, not its first, or close the card when another card's time falls inside it (unverified which reads right; pick the one where no card is ever out of clock order).
3. **Past days called "today" (116-009, 117-003): not in this ticket.** "Today's Workout" and "Intake today" are ratified in `energy-card.md`; Lee (2026-09-26) holds them for Xuan. No change.
4. **A finished day's projection meets "so far" (116-010).** `dashboard_assembler.dart:584` sets `digestionByEnd` to 10% of the target, even on a past day where eating is over. When the day is over, by-end digestion equals digestion so far (10% of what was eaten), so both columns agree. First check `docs/ssot/spec/.../intraday-display.md`. Today's projection stays as the spec has it.
5. **Skipped workouts on past days (117-002): won't fix.** The placement follows ratified S-7 (`macro-dashboard.md`). No change.
6. **Month picker Today closes the sheet, and the day arrows stay put (117-004).** `_goToToday` (`calendar_month_view_kyle.dart:399`) moves the selection but does not pop the sheet the way a day tap does. Make it pop. The header chevrons (`home_shell_chrome.dart`) sit right after the title, so they move with the day name's width. Give the title a fixed width, or pin the arrows to fixed slots.
7. **Pull to refresh syncs connected apps (117-005, Lee).** `macro_dashboard_screen.dart` has no `RefreshIndicator`. Add one whose pull calls `ActivitiesController.forceRefresh()` (`activities_controller.dart:481`, which goes through `IntegrationSyncCoordinator.forceSyncIntegrations` and skips the staleness check), then waits for the day to reload. Do not add horizontal day swipes: the surface spec forbids screen-level horizontal swipes.
8. **A sign-in opens on today (117-010).** `calendarSelectedDateProvider` (`calendar_selected_date_provider.dart`) is app-wide and never resets. Rebuild it on a user change: watch the signed-in user id in `build`, so sign-out, sign-in and a new account all start on today.
9. **Light theme Timeline is readable (119-003).** In Light, meal names, kcal and time labels vanish on cream, and the header and workout cards stay plum. The likely cause is `MeTokens` (`macro_dashboard/presentation/me_tokens.dart`) being dark-only constants (unverified). Route them through the `lib/theme/kyle_design/` registry so they follow the theme. Do not use `Color(0x…)` literals.
10. **Verified workout names show (100-002).** On a FinalSurge-verified card, the badge squeezes "Swim" to "SW…" (`macro_dashboard/presentation/widgets/workout_card.dart`, title near `:393`). The title gets space first. The badge shrinks, or wraps under the title.
11. **A provider-completed workout opens its detail (100-003, Lee).** `openActivityFuel` (`activities/presentation/navigation/open_activity_fuel.dart:12`) sends a plan-less activity to `/distancepacegut`, the Create New Activity Plan form. A completed activity (`status` completed, or a provider `completionType`) opens `/plan` view, never the form. Check that the view handles a missing plan (unverified).
12. **Location only for an upcoming activity with a forecast (100-004, Lee).** Opening a past workout asked for location. Item 11 removes this path. Also guard the input controllers' location request (`running_`/`cycling_`/`swimming_input_controller.dart`, via `location_service.dart`) so they ask only when the activity is upcoming and inside the forecast range.
13. **Clearing an override re-plans (116-003).** When Nutrition Targets clears a during override (`settings_controller.dart`), stored plans keep 50.4 g/h, and the info icon that explained the override is gone. Mark every not-yet-done activity whose stored plan used the override `needs_nutrition_refresh`, so it re-plans without it. While a stored plan still carries an override rate, keep the existing info icon (`activity_detail_screen.dart`, unverified where the icon's condition reads the current override instead of the plan's).
14. **By Hour counts sip-throughout items (116-005).** Items at `hourIndex -1` (sip throughout) add nothing to the per-hour lines, so Hour 1 and Hour 2 read 0 g and 0 mg. Spread each sip item evenly across the hours in the per-hour carb and sodium figures (`by_hour_view.dart`, `by_hour_sync_service.dart`). Numbers only. Rows and wording stay.
15. **Back from a new activity lands on the Timeline (116-011).** `showPlanAfterSuccessfulCreate` (`post_create_navigation.dart`) does `router.go('/main')` and then pushes the plan. Back still walked through Adjust Your Macros and the form. The likely cause is that part of the flow is pushed with `MaterialPageRoute` and never unwound by `go` (unverified). Find the imperative pushes, and make one Back from the new plan land on the Timeline.
16. **The during template passes its own carb check (117-016).** `during-template-solver.ts:856`: the best optimized candidate held 106 g against 95 g (112%, over the 1.1 cap at `:697`), so the plan fell back to the rule solver for a default 12 mi run. Reproduce with that input in `during-template-solver.test.ts`. Find why the search space has no candidate inside the cap (serving steps, or the sequential fill; unverified). Fix it so the template fits, or it logs why no template can. This is a fuelling-algorithm change: `docs/ssot/vectors/` (`fueling/during-workout-*.json`) must go green. Check the conformance baseline at HEAD first, because CF-2 was red at base before. Never edit `docs/ssot/`.
