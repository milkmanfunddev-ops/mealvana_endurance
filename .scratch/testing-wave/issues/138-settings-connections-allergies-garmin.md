1. **Garmin disconnect deregisters at Garmin (112-010, 121-010, Lee).** `GarminOAuthService.disconnect` (`garmin_oauth_service.dart:200`) asks `garmin-user-mapping` to delete only our row, so Garmin keeps pushing. In that function's `delete` action, read the mapping's token first. Refresh it if expired: move `ensureFreshGarminToken` out of `garmin-backfill/index.ts` into `_shared/garmin/`. Then call `DELETE https://apis.garmin.com/wellness-api/rest/user/registration` (documented in `docs/integration/api-exploration/index.html:1047`), and delete the row after that. A Garmin failure is logged, and the row is still deleted.
