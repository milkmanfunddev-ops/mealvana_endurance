**Decisions:**
- Non-food is the function's verdict, carried as `not_food` and answered as 422, the same contract the photo function and the client already share. The app never guesses from a zero-kcal item: a glass of water is food at 0 kcal.
- **Product question for Lee:** a describe call that found no food is charged one token (the model call succeeded; `debitForUsage` runs first). The photo function does not charge for non-food today: its 422 returns before its debit (`analyze-meal-photo/index.ts:276-279` vs `:308`). Either charge both or charge neither; this ticket leaves photo as it is and charges describe.
- The analysis lives in a controller scoped to Log a Meal, not in the Review route or the tab's widget state. Leaving Log a Meal (Close, or a logged meal) drops it; a new Analyze is charged.
- `Food.caloriesFor` changes the picker everywhere it is used (nutrition-plan swap, formula editor): a fractional quantity now shows the rounded figure, at most 1 kcal different from before.
- Not in this ticket: the other hardcoded strings on Review ("Review & Log", "Meal name", "Log this meal", "Failed to log meal…", `meal_review_screen.dart:145`, `:167`, `:185`, `:241`); the photo function's own not-food detection; 31-006 (remove without confirm).
