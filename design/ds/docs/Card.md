# Card

The base surface. On the blackberry ground a card is a faint cream-tinted fill with a hairline (never a solid box); on cream it's cream with a 1 px blackberry outline. 15 px radius (ratified), 16 px padding. `title` (Sansita 19) + `subtitle` (uppercase eyebrow). `accent` colours the border — electrolyte for verified/on-track.

```tsx
<Card title="Net Balance" subtitle="on track" accent="var(--me-electrolyte)">…</Card>
```
