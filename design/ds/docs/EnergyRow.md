# EnergyRow

A workout inside the Active Energy sheet: chip, Compadre Wide title with a `PLANNED` tag when not done, detail, kcal + chevron, and a provenance line under a hairline. Done rows are electrolyte-tinted.

```tsx
<EnergyRow title="12 mi Run" detail="5:57 AM · 5.0 mi · 43 min" kcal={411} status="verified" source="verified" sourceLabel="verified · Garmin · as planned" />
```

**Dart source (promote from):** `lib/features/macro_dashboard/presentation/widgets/breakdown_pager.dart`
