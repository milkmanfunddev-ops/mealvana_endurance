# SheetHeader

Top of every bottom sheet: cream close circle at the leading edge, centred Sansita title, muted time subtitle. Sheets are full-height on the blackberry ground and end with `PagerDots`.

```tsx
<SheetHeader title="Today's Energy" subtitle="6:14 PM · 76% of the day done" onClose={close} />
```
