Design a mobile camera capture screen for taking food photos in a nutrition tracking app. The user taps a camera icon and this screen opens to photograph their meal for AI recognition.

App style: Clean, modern fitness app. Orange accents. Dark overlay UI on camera feed.

CAMERA VIEW SCREEN:

1. Full-screen camera viewfinder showing a live preview of a meal on a table (plate with food visible)

2. TOP BAR (overlaid on camera, semi-transparent dark gradient):
   - Left: circular X/close button (36px, white icon on semi-transparent dark circle)
   - Right: flash toggle icon button (lightning bolt, white, same style)

3. BOTTOM AREA (overlaid, semi-transparent dark gradient from bottom):
   - Center: Large circular capture button (72px outer white ring, 60px inner white filled circle). Standard camera button style.
   - Bottom-left: Small rounded square (40px) showing last gallery photo thumbnail with thin white border. Tapping opens photo gallery picker.
   - Slight text above capture button: "Take a photo of your meal" in 13px white, semi-transparent

---

PHOTO PREVIEW SCREEN (after capture):

1. Full-screen display of the captured photo (the meal photo fills the screen, slight zoom-to-fit)

2. TOP BAR: semi-transparent dark gradient
   - Left: "Retake" text in white

3. BOTTOM AREA: semi-transparent dark gradient
   - Two buttons side by side with 16px gap, centered:
     - "Retake" - outlined button, white border, white text, rounded, 48px height, 45% width
     - "Use Photo" - filled orange button, white text, rounded, 48px height, 45% width
   - 32px bottom padding (above safe area)
