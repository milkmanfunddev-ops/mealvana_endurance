**As an athlete preparing for my race**, I want to choose from proven carb loading protocols so that I can follow a structured approach that matches my experience level and race distance.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- I can access carb loading protocols after creating a race day information or editing a race day information
- Multiple protocol options are available (2-day quick and 3-day classic)
- Each protocol displays expandable cards with detailed information, including a concise one-sentence introduction, a "Best For" section with tags indicating suitable race distances, research references to academic papers, and protocol phases outlining the key principles
- The amount of grams of carbs are customized based on my body weight, race distance, and the protocol I choose.
