# Privacy

domain: 9 Account and app · folder: `lib/features/privacy/` (1 file)

The first-run consent screen for athletes in the EEA, UK or Washington ("Your privacy · Share usage data", off by
default). Everything else about usage data lives under Settings → "Privacy" (`settings.md`). Consent is stored on the
phone (`analytics_consent_status/regime/at/version`).
