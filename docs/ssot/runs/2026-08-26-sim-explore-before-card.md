Reads as the design, not as a defect. Food names, "Hydration check" and the question in Sansita 700 sit comfortably with the Apercu body and the orange Compadre-style headers; nothing looks like a fallback font. The only visible cost is width — Sansita is wide, which is part of why names truncate (F-7).
