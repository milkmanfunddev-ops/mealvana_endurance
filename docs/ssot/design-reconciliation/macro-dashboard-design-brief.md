## Authority order, and the one rule

Specs govern: `qa/spec/daily-macros/` (incl. the ratified `intraday-display.md`) and
`qa/spec/fueling/` (incl. the ratified `post-workout.md`). This brief is their translation, not a
second source. Where the current prototype disagrees with this brief, the prototype loses.

**The traceability rule (Xuan): every number the design displays must be a quantity the specs
document. Mock *values* need not be accurate — the engine supplies accuracy at runtime — but they
must be *plausible outputs of the documented pipeline*, and every quantity-kind must exist in a
spec.** Do not invent new numbers, rows, or states; if a design idea needs a new quantity, it goes
back through reconciliation instead of into the mock.

## The changes (each cites its ruling)

**1 · One target field.** (F-03, Q-014) "Target" and "projected TDEE" are one quantity — show one
number everywhere; the burn-breakdown total visibly equals it. Exactly two labelled exception
states may show a difference: fat-at-floor ("target slightly above burn to protect minimum fat")
and `energy_basis: "pre_override"` ("we raised today's target to protect your energy
availability").

**2 · Conditional recovery copy.** (F-07, `post-workout.md` RATIFIED) Delete every unconditional
"within 30 minutes"/"within the hour". Two branches on `hours_to_next_session`: **< 8 h** → badge
"START NOW · refuel through the next 4 h", copy "Back at it within 8 hours — start refueling right
away and keep carbs coming for the next 4 hours." **≥ 24 h or unknown** → badge "WITH YOUR NEXT
MEAL", copy "No rush — your next normal meal covers it. Aim for your carb total today and ~20–30 g
of protein within a couple of hours." Unknown always takes the relaxed branch.

**3 · The three EA safety states.** (F-19) SOFT: dismissible **informational** banner — the
canonical mock day sits in SOFT; it must read as normal, not alarming. HARD: energy card shows the
raised target + explanation; surplus copy suppressed. BLOCK: **no plan renders at all** — full
screen, urgent but supportive health warning, zero macro numbers. These are core states, not edge
decorations.

**4 · Workout card states + "Mark as done".** (F27 confirmation ruling) Three visual states:
PLANNED (outlined/dim, planned chip) · DONE-verified (solid, verified chip) · DONE-confirmed
(solid, self-reported chip, "awaiting sync" glyph). Past the scheduled end without a sync, the card
offers one-tap **"Mark as done"** (optional duration adjust) — confirming keeps the workout's fuel
in today's plan; end-of-day with neither sync nor confirmation lapses to "skipped?" and the plan
reverts, never silently while on screen.

**5 · Sodium on during-ride items.** (F-20 — the numbers already exist in the ratified
`during-workout-sodium.md`) Each During-Ride row gains sodium ("Carb Drink Mix — 30 g carbs ·
500 ml · 410 mg sodium"). Do NOT add sodium to Before-Ride or daily rings — pre-workout sodium is
deliberately targetless.

**6 · Phase chip; no mode indicator.** (F-21, Q-005) Small chip near the date header (BASE /
BUILD / PEAK / TAPER / RACE WEEK / OFF-SEASON), tappable — it is the athlete's setting. When
phase is TAPER/PEAK and tomorrow is a race: one-line nudge "Racing tomorrow — consider Race
Week." Never show prospective/retrospective; "+18 vs plan" per-session deltas already carry it.

**7 · Scope the AI look-ahead copy to tomorrow.** (F-18) The weekly chart's Thu→Sat ramp is
correct and stays. The Wednesday card naming *Saturday* overreaches the engine: fueling
instructions may reference **tomorrow only**; multi-day mentions must read as schedule awareness,
not instruction.

**8 · Net-balance copy registers.** (`intraday-display.md` §2, ratified) Bands: ±200 "on track" ·
±200–500 "slight surplus/deficit" · beyond "surplus"/"deficit — time to eat". Two hard rules:
surplus copy never appears under `pre_override`; deficit copy is never congratulatory — it points
to food, not achievement.

**9 · Provenance chips per the pinned mapping.** (F-13) `GARMIN`→verified·Garmin ·
`TP_ACTUAL`→verified·TrainingPeaks · `MANUAL`→self-reported · `TP_PLANNED`/`TP_CALENDAR`→planned
(estimate) · `FORMULA`/`ZONE_DIST`→estimated. Total mapping — no other chips exist.
