Pre-workout carbohydrate — the 15-minute input grid

The plan total is BW × min(hours, 4) — v1's 0.5 g/kg floor removed. It is split 60 / 30 / 10 when a meal window exists and 75 / 25 when it does not. The app takes lead time in 15-minute steps up to 4 hours, so these seventeen columns are every plan the product can produce at a given body weight. Engine values are exact; the drawer rounds to 5 g.
