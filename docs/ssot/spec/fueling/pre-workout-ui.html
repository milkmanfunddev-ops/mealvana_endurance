Mealvana QA · pre-workout interface

A live tally, and an info tab behind it

Add and remove food in the mockup — the meters move. Fill is what your food delivers, the white tick is what we suggest, the ends are the permissible range. Everything explaining where the number came from sits behind the ⓘ. Ranges stay on the screen because they are the feedback; provenance, formulas and citations are not.
