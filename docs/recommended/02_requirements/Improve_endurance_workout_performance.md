# Improve endurance workout performance and comfort through better nutrition and carb-loading practices

![nutrition-opportunity-tree.svg](attachment:4feb588c-3dba-46da-8a73-63a166e8eddd:nutrition-opportunity-tree.svg)

## 1. **Optimize Performance Nutrition**

### 1.1 **Fuel appropriately before the workouts**

- Understand optimal carb-loading time window (24 vs 48 hrs)
- Know when to reduce fiber intake to minimize GI distress
- Identify ideal macronutrient ratios for carb-loading
- Translate the nutritional needs into meal ideas

### 1.2 **Fuel appropriately during the workouts**

- Know exactly how much nutrients (carbs, water, electrolyte, etc.) to consume per hour and the total amount for my body size, workout intensity, duration, and other factors (such as weather or the phase of training)
- Translate the nutrients needs into products and / or recipes
- Timing of consumption
- Adjust for temperature, humidity, altitude
- Carrying capacity calculations
- Product concentration optimization

### 1.3 **Fuel appropriately after the workouts**

- Identify ideal macronutrient ratios for recovery
- Timing of recovery nutrition
    - Meet 20-40g protein window within 30-40 minutes post-workout
- Translate the nutritional needs into meal ideas
- Quick practical recovery solutions

## 2. **Simplify Tracking & Planning**

### 2.1 **Make nutrition logging effortless**

- Use voice notes to describe what I ate and how I felt
- Take photos instead of manual entry
- Import workout data automatically from other apps
- Set reminders for nutrition timing during long workouts
- Log without interrupting workout flow
- Import professional test results (sweat tests)
- Bridge training vs race day data gap

### 2.2 **Get actionable insights without overwhelm**

- See if I hit my carb targets in simple visual format
- Track patterns between nutrition and performance
- Get summaries of what worked/didn't work
    - Compare this week's long run nutrition to last week's
    - Identify foods that consistently work well for me
    - Flag when I'm consistently under-fueling

### 2.3 **Control and customize my nutrition plan**

- Adjust carb/hour targets based on my experience
- Set custom timing for when to take nutrition
- Add foods not in any database (homemade items)
- Override app suggestions with personal preferences
- Export plans to share with coaches or training partners
- Allow informed deviation from recommendations
- Support alternative timing strategies (by distance not time)

### 2.4 Communicating my nutrition plan

- I want my coach to know my nutrition plan and my execution
- I want a dietitian to see my nutrition plan and my execution
- Share data with medical professionals

## 3. **Personalize Through Experimentation & Learning**

### 3.1 **Discover what works for my unique body**

- Determine personal fiber thresholds before events
- Time bowel movements optimally before workouts
- Track correlations between Friday carb loading and Saturday performance
- Identify trigger foods that cause distress
- Work with a sport medicine doctor to conduct sweat test
- Evaluate my hydration through images of my urine
- Extreme physiological variations (heavy sweaters, high sodium loss)
- DIY sweat test protocols
- Progressive gut training protocols
- Pattern recognition for fatigue points (2/3 wall)
- Product texture/taste/temperature preferences
- Pre-run timing for optimal digestion/bathroom routine
- Non-traditional carb sources (fruit vs grains)

### 3.2 **Address psychological and social challenges**

- Recognize when fear of carbs limits performance
- Navigate social pressure to diet while training
- Normalize eating large amounts during heavy training
- Psychological preference for feeling "light”

### 3.3 **Build evidence-based understanding**

- Access research on endurance nutrition in digestible formats
- Understand why carbs are essential for performance
- Learn how underfueling impacts training adaptations
- Get clarity on protein needs for endurance athletes
- Understand gut training and absorption limits
- Learn from credible experts and sports nutritionists
