Get the most out of every session. Mealvana Endurance turns every run, ride, and race into a fueling plan you can actually follow—exact carbs, fluids, and sodium, timed before, during, and after, and tracked live so you always know what to take and when.

Built for runners, cyclists, and triathletes who are done guessing.

WHAT YOU GET
- Personalized fueling targets in grams per hour, tuned to your session, your sweat, and the weather.
- Live in-race tracking—check off fuel as you go so you always know what you've taken.
- Train your gut: watch your carbs-per-hour climb, run after run, as you build tolerance.
- A 3-day carb-load and race-day plan for your key events.
- A dietitian-curated formula library you can customize and pin to any workout.
- Periodized daily nutrition with energy-availability alerts, so you fuel the work—not under-fuel it.

LOG IN SECONDS
- 60,000+ searchable, scannable products at your fingertips.

SYNCS WITH YOUR TRAINING
- Pulls your workouts from Garmin, TrainingPeaks, Final Surge, and VDOT—or build your own.

Stop leaving your nutrition to chance. Fuel every session like it counts.
