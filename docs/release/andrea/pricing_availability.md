Preparing for Review: Pricing and Availability
In this lesson, we’ll cover how to set your app’s pricing and availability in App Store Connect. These settings determine how much users will pay for your app and which countries or regions your app will be available in. You’ll also have the option to customize pricing for different regions and manage availability for specific platforms like Apple Silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro.

Let’s dive into the details.

Pricing
To set your app’s pricing, navigate to App Store > Monetization from the side menu:

Pricing and Availability
Click Add Pricing, then choose your desired price from the dropdown:

App price
On the next page, you can set different prices for individual countries or regions:

Country or Region Prices
Once you’ve made your selections, click Confirm to finalize the pricing:

Confirm App Pricing
App Availability
Next, click on Set Up Availability to choose where your app will be available:

App Availability
Select your desired option and click Next:

App Availability
After confirming your selection, you’ll see a summary of your availability settings:

App Availability
Platform-Specific Availability
At the bottom of the Pricing and Availability page, you’ll find additional options for Apple Silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro:

Apple Silicon Macs, Apple Vision Pro
If you haven’t optimized your app for these platforms, I recommend deselecting the corresponding options to avoid user experience issues. Once you’ve made your changes, click Save.

Preparing for Review: Summary
Return to the main overview page and click Add for Review:

Unable to Add for Review
If you see the message “Unable to Add for Review”, don’t panic! This message usually appears if you haven’t yet selected a build for submission. Double-check the list of reasons to ensure you’ve completed all the required steps.
