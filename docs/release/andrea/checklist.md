iOS App Release Checklist
As we’ve seen in this module, releasing an iOS app on the App Store is a complex matter.

Here’s a high-level checklist you can use to navigate the entire process:

iOS App Release Checklist ✅
Each of these items involves multiple steps. Refer to the individual lessons for detailed guidance:

Register a new App ID in your Apple Developer account
Create a new iOS app in App Store Connect and add all the metadata, screenshots, and app review details
Add the App information, Age Ratings, and App Encryption Documentation
Declare the App Privacy and Data Collection Practices
Set your app’s Pricing and Availability
Add a Privacy Manifest in Xcode
Review the Xcode Project Settings
Build, upload, and submit your app to App Store Connect
