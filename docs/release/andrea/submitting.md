Submitting Your iOS App to App Store Connect
In this lesson, you’ll learn how to submit your app for review, address potential compliance issues, and choose whether to release your app manually or automatically after approval.

By the end of this lesson, your app will be in the review queue, and you’ll be one step closer to getting it live on the App Store! 🚀

Prerequisite
Make sure you’ve already uploaded a build to App Store Connect, either manually or using a CI/CD pipeline. For details, see the previous lesson.

Submitting your app for review
Go to your app in App Store Connect, scroll down to Build, and click on the + icon:

Select your build, then click Done:

Your app will now enter Apple’s review queue. You’ll receive a notification when the review is complete, and if everything checks out, your app will be ready for release!

Wrapping Up
Congratulations! You’ve now successfully submitted your iOS app to App Store Connect. This is the final step before your app is reviewed and (hopefully) approved for release on the App Store. 🎉
