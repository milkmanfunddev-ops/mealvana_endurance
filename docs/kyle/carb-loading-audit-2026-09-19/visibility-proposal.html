Don’t add a card. Change the card’s face.

The energy card is already one component with three faces, chosen by the surface, showing the single most decision-relevant number for the lens. On a loading day that number is grams of carbohydrate. So carb loading doesn’t need a new element on the dashboard — it needs a fourth face.

Verdict

Three reasons yours beats mine

It never scrolls away

My banner sat at the head of the timeline, so it left the screen on the first scroll. The energy card is inside the pinned instrument block — Ruling #4 — so the load stays on screen for the whole day, which is exactly what a three-day protocol needs.

It adds nothing to the surface

No new element, no new rail ink, no height taken from the timeline, no new gesture. The surface’s composition table is unchanged. What changes is one component’s face set — a smaller, cleaner thing for a ratifier to rule on than a new dashboard citizen.

The spec already argues for it

P-2: “collapsed is the single most decision-relevant number for that lens.” On a loading day, net kcal is not that number — 232 g to go is. Your proposal isn’t bending the contract, it’s applying it.

Two things your redirect caught

Corrections to my first pass

Yolk fails conformance

I coloured the first mockup in yolk. The token registry says it is RESERVED — no meaning contract yet, ruled 2026-08-25, and “a component using it fails conformance.” Carb-loading progress is daily intake, so Q-D3 puts it in orange — and that is now every carb figure in the mock. Target-met stays orange too: electrolyte means the burn side and per-workout fuel, never daily intake.

The card can’t navigate

Gesture E1 says tapping the card “never navigates”. Since the load face doesn’t expand either, the whole route to detail runs through Full Breakdown (E2) — which is why it is promoted onto the collapsed face rather than living inside an expansion that no longer exists.

Ruled 2026-09-19

No expansion — the breakdown does that work

The load face is collapsed-only. Two states of a number, one of them a half-measure, is one state too many when the day has a single job. So Full Breakdown moves up onto the face itself, and everything that would have lived in an expansion — protein, fat, kcal, the per-meal split — lives on the breakdown page instead.

What it costs in the contract

E1 is suppressed on this face — the same shape as the workout card’s G3, so there is precedent for a gesture going quiet in one state. The chevron disappears rather than sitting there doing nothing.

P-1 needs one line

“Expansion persists across face switches.” A face with no expansion has to remember rather than clear it: expand Meals, switch to All, switch back — it should still be open. The mock does this; the spec should say it.

The breakdown page is the next design

It now carries everything: macros, the meal-by-meal split, the three days. It is also the natural place for the way into logging — which raises the question below, and is the piece worth designing next.

Before anyone writes code

What needs your ruling

  1. S-5 The surface scope guard still bites. “No phase indicator, no safety states, no sodium on this surface — deferred, not forgotten.” DAY 2 OF 3 is a phase indicator. The ask is smaller than it was — amend S-5 to admit a fuelling-phase face on the energy card, rather than to admit a new surface citizen — but it is still the one hard blocker.
  2. P-3 The to-go copy needs a register. Every string on this card traces to intraday-display.md §2, verbatim. “232 g to go” has no register yet. Either a loading band register gets written, or the collapsed face carries only the number and no copy.
  3. Is the breakdown page the loading page? The other three faces open a sheet — Today’s Energy, Active Energy, Today’s Fuel. If the load’s breakdown is a fourth sheet, the athlete still needs a separate route to the screen where they actually log food. If the breakdown is that screen, the feature collapses to two surfaces: a face on the dashboard and one loading page. That is the simplest thing that works, and it is the decision that shapes the next design.
  4. Fourth face, or a variant of MEALS? I went with a fourth face because MEALS collapsed is a kcal budget, and a loading day’s orienting number is one macro against a therapeutic target — a genuinely different quantity. Reusing MEALS with a conditional variant is defensible and cheaper, at the cost of making that face’s goldens conditional.
  5. Goldens go 6 → 8. Three faces × two expansion states becomes four × two. The spec change lands first, then the goldens — the conformance section is explicit that a golden may only be regenerated after the spec changes.
  6. Does the Meals face show the loading target? On a loading day the C in DAILY BUDGET · C · P · F is 544 g rather than a normal day’s number. That is either a quiet, correct consequence or a second place the load leaks in. Your call whether it needs saying.
  7. Two races, overlapping loads. Still open from the first pass, and now sharper: the face can only show one DAY n OF 3.
