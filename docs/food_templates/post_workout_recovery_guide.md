## What the Experts Say

### Dr. Rachel Mitchell, PhD (Nutrition Science)

Dr. Mitchell is a nutrition science advisor for Mealvana. Her rule for post-workout recovery:

> "Any combo works well as long as both a carb source and protein source is covered. The 3-4:1 ratio of carbs to protein is ideal."

Her recommended post-workout formulas:

| Carb Source | Protein Source |
|:--|:--|
| Chocolate milk | (built-in) |
| Granola + honey + berries | Greek yogurt |
| Banana + peanut butter + graham crackers | Protein shake |
| Any fruit | Protein shake |
| Granola bar | Protein shake |
| Toast + jam | Eggs |
| Applesauce | Cottage cheese |
| Applesauce + graham crackers | Protein shake |
| PB&J sandwich | Glass of milk |
| Crackers + jam | Cheese stick |
| Oatmeal + honey (or maple syrup) + banana | Protein powder + almond butter |
| Fruit smoothie | Greek yogurt + protein powder |
| Bagel + cream cheese | Turkey slices |
| Waffle + banana | Protein shake |
| Toast + honey | Protein shake |
| Crackers | Turkey + cheese roll-ups |

### What Pro Athletes Eat After Training

**Jan Frodeno** (3x Ironman World Champion): Banana + protein powder + milk + peanut butter blended. Same smoothie every single day.

**Lucy Charles-Barclay** (Ironman World Champion): Protein shake within 30 minutes of every session -- a team member has it waiting at the finish line for races.

**Lionel Sanders** (Pro Triathlete): Core Power protein shakes for travel convenience. Smoothie bowls at home. Reformed sugar intake after bloodwork showed pre-diabetic levels despite world-class fitness.

**Sam Long** (Pro Triathlete): Eggs. "The single food item he could not live without." Same recovery foods daily to eliminate decision fatigue.

**Cody Beals** (Pro Vegan Triathlete): Vega Sport plant protein shake immediately after every session.

## The 10 Recovery Templates

Every template includes a carb source and a protein source. Hydration (water) and electrolytes are added separately by the system based on the athlete's sweat rate and conditions.

---

### Template 1: Chocolate Milk + Banana + Protein Powder

*The most studied recovery combination. Protein powder mixed into the milk closes the protein gap for larger athletes or longer sessions.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Chocolate Milk | 1 cup (8 oz) | 30 g | 9 g | 160 mg | 3 cups |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g | 1 mg | 2 |
| Protein Powder | 1 scoop | 3 g | 24 g | 130 mg | 2 scoops |

**Scaling:** Chocolate milk and banana scale together to hit carb targets. Protein powder is fixed at 1 scoop for most athletes (2 scoops only for 90+ kg athletes after long sessions).

**Allergens:** dairy | **Excludes:** vegan, paleo

---

### Template 2: Protein Shake + Banana + Graham Crackers

*Grab-and-go from any convenience store. No blender, no cooking. Graham crackers deliver satisfying crunch after hours of gels and liquids.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Protein Shake (RTD bottle) | 1 bottle (11 oz) | 5 g | 25 g | 200 mg | 1 bottle |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g | 1 mg | 2 |
| Graham Crackers | 2 sheets | 21.5 g | 2 g | 169 mg | 2 servings |

**Scaling:** Protein shake is always 1 bottle (fixed). Banana and graham crackers scale up for higher carb targets. A 91 kg athlete after a long ride might get 2 bananas + 4 sheets of graham crackers alongside the same 1 bottle.

**Allergens:** dairy, gluten | **Excludes:** gluten-free

---

### Template 3: Recovery Protein Drink + Pretzels

*Zero prep. Recovery protein drink (like Core Power) from a gas station cooler plus pretzels. Lionel Sanders' travel go-to.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Recovery Protein Drink | 1 bottle (14 oz) | 9 g | 42 g | 250 mg | 3 bottles |
| Pretzels | 1 oz | 23 g | 2.8 g | 385 mg | 4 oz |

**Scaling:** These scale independently. The drink is typically 1 bottle (protein is already met). Pretzels scale freely to hit carb targets -- a bigger athlete or longer workout just means more pretzels. For very long sessions (3+ hr), a second bottle can be added.

**Allergens:** dairy, gluten | **Excludes:** vegan, paleo, gluten-free

---

### Template 4: Plant Protein Shake + Banana + Pretzels

*Fully vegan, grab-and-go. Cody Beals' approach -- plant protein shake immediately after every session.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Plant Protein Shake (RTD bottle) | 1 bottle (11 oz) | 10 g | 20 g | 320 mg | 1 bottle |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g | 1 mg | 2 |
| Pretzels | 1 oz | 23 g | 2.8 g | 385 mg | 4 oz |

**Scaling:** Shake is fixed at 1 bottle. Banana and pretzels scale independently to hit carb targets. After a short easy workout, might just be 1 banana + no pretzels. After a long hard session, 2 bananas + 2-3 oz pretzels.

**Allergens:** gluten | **Excludes:** gluten-free | **Vegan-friendly**

---

### Template 5: Yogurt + Granola + Fruit + Honey + Protein Powder

*The parfait. Assemble in a mason jar the night before and grab from the fridge. Protein powder stirred into yogurt ensures the protein target is met.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Yogurt (plain) | 1 cup | 17.4 g | 8.5 g | 172 mg | 2 cups |
| Granola | 1/4 cup | 17.5 g | 3 g | 7 mg | 2 servings |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g | 1 mg | 2 |
| Honey | 1 tbsp | 17.3 g | 0.1 g | 1 mg | 2 tbsp |
| Protein Powder | 1 scoop | 3 g | 24 g | 130 mg | 2 scoops |

**Scaling:** Yogurt, granola, banana, and honey scale together proportionally (bigger parfait). Protein powder is fixed at 1 scoop. Swap banana for mixed berries, blueberries, or any seasonal fruit.

**Allergens:** dairy, gluten | **Excludes:** vegan, paleo

---

### Template 6: Oatmeal + Protein Powder + Banana + Honey

*Warm and comforting. Add hot water, stir in protein powder, top with banana and honey. Dr. Mitchell recommends multiple oatmeal variations.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Oatmeal (1/2 cup dry) | 1 serving | 27 g | 5 g | 0 mg | 2 servings |
| Protein Powder | 1 scoop | 3 g | 24 g | 130 mg | 2 scoops |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g | 1 mg | 2 |
| Honey | 1 tbsp | 17.3 g | 0.1 g | 1 mg | 2 tbsp |

**Scaling:** Oatmeal, banana, and honey scale together (bigger bowl). Protein powder is fixed at 1 scoop. Swap honey for maple syrup. Add almond butter or peanut butter for extra calories and healthy fats.

**Allergens:** dairy, gluten | **Excludes:** vegan (use plant protein powder for vegan version), gluten-free

---

### Template 7: PB&J + Milk

*Under $2 total. Allen Lim (Skratch Labs) calls PB&J "the perfect real food recovery." Dr. Mitchell lists both "PB&J sandwich" and "PB&J + glass of milk."*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Toast / Bread | 1 slice | 12.7 g | 1.8 g | 130 mg | 2 slices |
| Peanut Butter | 1 tbsp | 3.6 g | 3.6 g | 73 mg | 2 tbsp |
| Jam / Jelly | 1 tbsp | 13.8 g | 0.1 g | 6 mg | 2 tbsp |
| Milk (whole) | 1 cup (8 oz) | 12 g | 8 g | 105 mg | 3 cups |

**Scaling:** All components scale together as a unit. A bigger athlete gets a bigger sandwich (2 slices, 2 tbsp PB, 2 tbsp jam) and more milk. Think of it as "number of PB&J sandwiches + glasses of milk."

**Allergens:** gluten, peanuts, dairy | **Excludes:** vegan, nut-free

---

### Template 8: Bagel + Cream Cheese + Turkey

*Savory recovery for athletes who crave salt after hard sessions. Dense carbs, lean protein, and lots of sodium.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Bagel (large) | 1 large | 53 g | 11 g | 462 mg | 2 |
| Cream Cheese | 1 tbsp | 0.8 g | 0.9 g | 47 mg | 2 tbsp |
| Turkey (deli) | 3 oz | 2 g | 15 g | 744 mg | 2 servings |

**Scaling:** All components scale together. One bagel = one serving of cream cheese + one serving of turkey. A 91 kg athlete after a long workout might get 1.5 bagels (or 1 large + half) with proportionally more cream cheese and turkey.

**Allergens:** gluten, dairy | **Excludes:** vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free

---

### Template 9: Toast + Egg + Jam

*The morning workout recovery breakfast. Sam Long's eggs are his #1 recovery food. Quick to prepare -- boil eggs in advance or scramble in 3 minutes.*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Toast / Bread | 1 slice | 12.7 g | 1.8 g | 130 mg | 2 slices |
| Egg (boiled/poached) | 1 large | 1 g | 6.1 g | 62 mg | 2 eggs |
| Jam / Jelly | 1 tbsp | 13.8 g | 0.1 g | 6 mg | 2 tbsp |

**Scaling:** All components scale together. More toast = more eggs = more jam. Two slices of toast with 2 eggs and 2 tbsp jam is the standard "full serving."

**Allergens:** gluten, eggs | **Excludes:** vegan | **Dairy-free**

---

### Template 10: Waffle + Banana + Protein Shake

*Fun and fast. Pop waffles in the toaster, grab a shake from the fridge, eat a banana. Done in 2 minutes. Dr. Mitchell's "waffle + protein shake + banana."*

| Food | Serving | Carbs | Protein | Sodium | Max Servings |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Toaster Waffle | 1 waffle | 15.4 g | 2 g | 212 mg | 2 waffles |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g | 1 mg | 2 |
| Protein Shake (RTD bottle) | 1 bottle (11 oz) | 5 g | 25 g | 200 mg | 1 bottle |

**Scaling:** Waffles and banana scale together to hit carb targets. Protein shake is always 1 bottle (fixed). A bigger target means more waffles and/or an extra banana alongside the same single bottle.

**Allergens:** gluten, eggs, dairy | **Excludes:** vegan, gluten-free

## How Templates Scale: Athlete Examples

The following examples show exactly what different athletes would be served based on their body weight, workout type, and dietary needs. Portions are calculated by the solver to hit personalized carb and protein targets.

---

### Example 1: Light Runner, Short Easy Run

**Sarah -- 54 kg, 45-min easy run, no restrictions**

Targets: ~54 g carbs, 20 g protein

**Template 1: Chocolate Milk + Banana + Protein Powder**

| Food | Servings | Carbs | Protein |
|:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Chocolate Milk | 1 cup | 30 g | 9 g |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27 g | 1.3 g |
| Protein Powder | 1 scoop | 3 g | 24 g |
| **Total** | | **60 g** | **34.3 g** |

Light and quick. One cup of chocolate milk, one banana, one scoop of protein stirred in. Done in 60 seconds.

---

### Example 2: Large Cyclist, Long Hard Ride

**Marcus -- 91 kg, 4-hr hard ride, no restrictions**

Two full bagel sandwiches. Everything scales together -- more bagel means proportionally more cream cheese and turkey.

## Scaling Rules Summary

### Fixed vs. Scaling Components

| Component Type | Behavior | Examples |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **RTD Protein Drinks** | Fixed at 1 bottle for most scenarios. Max 1 bottle for protein_shake and plant_protein_shake. Recovery protein drink allows up to 3 for extreme cases. | Protein Shake, Plant Protein Shake, Recovery Protein Drink |
| **Protein Powder** | Fixed at 1 scoop for most athletes. 2 scoops only for athletes 90+ kg after sessions over 2.5 hr. | Protein Powder (whey or plant) |
| **Carb Foods** | Scale to hit carb target. More food for bigger athletes and longer workouts. | Banana, graham crackers, pretzels, oatmeal, toast, bagel, waffle, granola, jam, honey |
| **Combo Foods** | Scale with the carb target. | Chocolate milk (carbs + protein), yogurt (carbs + protein), milk (carbs + protein) |

### Linked vs. Independent Scaling

| Template | Scaling Mode | What This Means |
|:--|:-:|:--|
| 1. Choc Milk + Banana + Protein | Linked carbs, fixed protein | Milk and banana grow together. Protein powder stays at 1 scoop. |
| 2. Shake + Banana + Grahams | Fixed protein, linked carbs | Shake = 1 bottle always. Banana and grahams grow together. |
| 3. Recovery Drink + Pretzels | Independent | Drink = 1 bottle. Pretzels scale freely to hit carbs. |
| 4. Plant Shake + Banana + Pretzels | Fixed protein, independent carbs | Shake = 1 bottle. Banana and pretzels each scale as needed. |
| 5. Yogurt Parfait | Linked carbs, fixed protein | Yogurt, granola, fruit, honey grow as a unit. Protein powder = 1 scoop. |
| 6. Oatmeal + Protein + Banana | Linked carbs, fixed protein | Oatmeal, banana, honey grow as a unit. Protein powder = 1 scoop. |
| 7. PB&J + Milk | All linked | Everything scales together. Bigger sandwich = more of everything. |
| 8. Bagel + CC + Turkey | All linked | Everything scales together. More bagel = more toppings. |
| 9. Toast + Egg + Jam | All linked | Everything scales together. More toast = more eggs = more jam. |
| 10. Waffle + Banana + Shake | Fixed protein, linked carbs | Shake = 1 bottle. Waffles and banana grow together. |
