## Why this matters
This is the single largest divergence between what ships and what Xuan wrote (D-001) — the moment-based voice
contract (`spec/agent/voice.md`) is a from-scratch interpretation of a real tension in Xuan's own sources
(clipped persona spec vs. exclamation-heavy scenarios), not a mechanical build against unambiguous text.

## The question
Does the moment-dependent voice register (`spec/agent/voice.md`: PICKING ≤2 sentences · PRESENTING 2–4 ·
EXPLAINING uncapped · MILESTONE one exclamation allowed; emoji ban kept throughout) correctly split the
difference between Xuan's clipped shipped persona and her warm, celebratory scenario writing — or does she want
something else entirely?

## Options
- **Confirm the moment-based register as-built.** Terse while picking, warm while presenting/explaining/
  celebrating. Splits the difference; each register is one block Xuan can strike individually rather than an
  all-or-nothing tone call.
- **Reject in favor of something closer to the scenarios.** More warmth throughout, not just at milestones —
  would mean loosening the PICKING register too.
- **Reject in favor of the original clipped persona.** Revert to the flat two-sentence cap everywhere — would
  undo D-001's fix but keep AI cost/latency lower (shorter outputs).

## Recommendation
Ship the moment-based register and let usage data (the vana-eval transcripts, real dev conversations) inform
whether any one register needs adjustment, rather than trying to resolve the tension analytically before any
athlete has seen it.
