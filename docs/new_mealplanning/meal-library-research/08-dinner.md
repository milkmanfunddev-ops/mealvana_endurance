### Chicken, jasmine rice & roasted broccoli with teriyaki
- meal_type: dinner
- context: everyday
- ingredients: chicken breast 200g, jasmine rice (dry) 100g, broccoli 200g, teriyaki sauce 2 tbsp, sesame oil 1 tsp
- diets_ok: omnivore, pescatarian (swap chicken), low_carb (reduce rice)
- allergens: soy, sesame
- swaps: teriyaki sauce (tamari-based)→check label for gluten-free tamari (gluten-free); soy sauce→coconut aminos (soy-free)
- approx_macros: ~650 kcal, 85g carbs, 45g protein, 12g fat
- prep: batch — cook Sunday, 4 servings
- source: pro triathlete Chris Lieto's stated dinner buffet of "chicken, broccoli, rice" (Triathlete.com "Fuel Like a Pro") — https://www.triathlete.com/nutrition/race-fueling/fuel-like-a-pro/
- why: the archetypal protein+carb+veg triathlete plate, batch-cooks and reheats cleanly
