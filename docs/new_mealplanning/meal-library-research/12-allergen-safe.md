### Sunflower butter, banana & oat toast (nut-free)
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: everyday, pre-session
- ingredients: whole grain toast 2 slices, sunflower seed butter 2 tbsp, banana 1, honey 1 tsp, cinnamon pinch
- diets_ok: omnivore, vegetarian, pescatarian
- allergens: gluten (bread — use GF bread to remove)
- swaps: bread→certified GF bread (gluten-free, still nut/egg/soy/dairy-free); honey→maple syrup (vegan)
- approx_macros: ~430 kcal, 65g carbs, 10g protein, 15g fat
- prep: 5 min
- source: SunButter FAQs (sunbutter.com/pages/faqs) — sunflower butter marketed explicitly as free of all top-9 allergens; toast+nut-butter+banana is the standard peanut-butter-toast swap widely reported in allergy-athlete blogs
- why: classic pre-run carb+fat combo with zero peanut/tree-nut risk, works for the largest single allergen group (nut allergy)
