### Ugali with beef stew & managu (Kipchoge lunch/dinner staple)
- meal_type: dinner
- context: everyday, rest-day
- ingredients: maize meal (ugali) 200g dry weight, lean beef 120g, managu or sukuma wiki (African nightshade/kale) 150g, onion, tomato, oil 1 tsp
- diets_ok: omnivore
- allergens: none
- swaps: beef→lentils/beans for vegetarian/vegan version (githeri-style)
- approx_macros: ~650 kcal, 90g carbs, 35g protein, 15g fat
- prep: batch — cook stew ahead, ugali fresh 15 min
- source: reported staple for Eliud Kipchoge and Kaptagat camp runners — Olympics.com (https://www.olympics.com/en/news/what-olympic-marathon-hero-eliud-kipchoge-eats), Sweat Elite (https://articles.sweatelite.co/eliud-kipchoge-diet/)
- why: high-carb starch base + iron-rich beef + greens is the literal daily template of the winningest distance-running culture on earth
