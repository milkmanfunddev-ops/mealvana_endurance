- General knowledge of naturally GF/DF staples cross-checked across the above: rice, potatoes, sweet potatoes, corn tortillas, GF oats, quinoa, rice noodles/soba (check 100% buckwheat for GF), buckwheat, polenta.

Note: most published "day of eating" pieces for celiac/GF pro endurance athletes (Heather Wurtele, Jordan Nichols) describe *principles* (safe-food rotation, avoiding eating out, naturally GF whole foods) rather than itemized meals — attributed as "commonly reported" where a specific dish wasn't named by the athlete.

### GF oats & banana bowl with almond butter and berries
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: everyday, pre-session
- ingredients: certified GF rolled oats 80g, almond milk 250ml, banana 1, almond butter 1 tbsp, blueberries 60g, cinnamon
- diets_ok: vegetarian, vegan, mediterranean
- allergens: tree_nuts
- swaps: almond butter→sunflower seed butter (tree_nut-free); almond milk→oat milk (if oats not the allergen concern)
- approx_macros: ~520 kcal, 78g carb, 14g protein, 17g fat
- prep: 5 min
- source: trigirl.co.uk GF/DF triathlete breakfast list ("GF porridge oats... with almond or soya milk") + runtothefinish.com GF runner plan (nut butter with GF options); commonly reported staple for celiac/DF endurance athletes
- why: naturally gluten-free and dairy-free carb-forward breakfast that fits the pre-session low-fibre, low-fat brief
