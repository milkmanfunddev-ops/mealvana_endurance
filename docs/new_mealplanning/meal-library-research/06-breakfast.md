### Overnight oats with yogurt, nuts & berries
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: everyday, rest-day, travel
- ingredients: rolled oats 70g, Activia-style yogurt 150g, mixed nuts 20g, milk 150ml, honey 1 tbsp, mixed berries 80g
- diets_ok: omnivore, vegetarian, mediterranean
- allergens: dairy, tree_nuts
- swaps: yogurt→coconut yogurt + milk→oat milk (vegan, dairy-free); oats→certified GF oats (gluten-free); omit nuts (tree_nut-free)
- approx_macros: ~480 kcal, 65g carbs, 18g protein, 15g fat
- prep: 5 min night-before, no-cook morning
- source: Kristian Blummenfelt (Norwegian Method) — overnight oats with Activia yogurt and mixed nuts, prepped the day before (nutri.it.com/what-diet-does-kristian-blummenfelt-follow; vo2master.com Norwegian Method interview)
- why: no-cook prep fits early race-morning/travel logistics; matches what a reigning Olympic/world-champion triathlete actually eats

### Classic porridge with banana & peanut butter
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: pre-session, everyday
- ingredients: rolled oats 80g, water or milk 300ml, banana 1, peanut butter 1 tbsp, honey 1 tbsp
- diets_ok: omnivore, vegetarian, vegan (if made with water/plant milk)
- allergens: dairy (if milk used), peanuts
- swaps: milk→water or oat milk (vegan, dairy-free); peanut butter→sunflower seed butter (peanut-free); oats→certified GF oats (gluten-free)
- approx_macros: ~520 kcal, 85g carbs, 15g protein, 14g fat
- prep: 5 min
- source: widely reported pro-triathlete staple — "toast with nut butter and honey" and "oats or granola" were the two most common pro-breakfast answers (active.com/triathlon/what-do-pro-triathletes-eat-for-breakfast); Frezer Legesse eats oatmeal+banana+Greek yogurt 3h pre-race (rei.com)
- why: low-fibre-enough, high-carb, familiar — textbook pre-key-session breakfast

### Baked oatmeal squares (batch)
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: everyday, rest-day, travel
- ingredients: rolled oats 250g, mashed banana 2, eggs 2, milk 300ml, cinnamon 1 tsp, baking powder 1 tsp, chopped walnuts 40g, dried fruit 40g
- diets_ok: omnivore, vegetarian
- allergens: eggs, dairy, tree_nuts, gluten (oats)
- swaps: eggs→flax egg + milk→oat milk (vegan, egg/dairy-free); oats→certified GF oats (gluten-free); omit walnuts (tree_nut-free)
- approx_macros: ~380 kcal per portion (1/8 batch), 55g carbs, 12g protein, 11g fat
- prep: batch — bake Sunday, cuts into 8 portions, reheats in 1 min
- source: standard batch-cook staple adapted from oatmeal-bake format popular in triathlon meal-prep content (beginnertriathlete.com oatmeal guidance; commonly reported meal-prep format across r/triathlon)
- why: grab-and-go breakfast for a training week, freezes well, hits carb target without a morning cook session

### EF Pro Cycling rice cakes (savoury, egg & bacon)
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: pre-session, race-week, travel
- ingredients: cooked jasmine or sushi rice 200g, eggs 2 (scrambled), bacon 2 rashers, soy sauce 1 tbsp, sugar 1 tsp
- diets_ok: omnivore
- allergens: eggs, soy
- swaps: bacon→smoked tofu, soy sauce→tamari (gluten-free version); omit bacon (pescatarian-adjacent, still has eggs)
- approx_macros: ~450 kcal, 55g carbs, 22g protein, 14g fat
- prep: batch — cook rice ahead, assemble 10 min, wraps portably
- source: Allen Lim (Feed Zone Cookbook) / EF Pro Cycling team recipe — pro peloton on-the-bike and pre-ride staple (efprocycling.com/tips-recipes/team-recipe-on-the-bike-rice-cakes; outsideonline.com/food/pelotons-power-food)
- why: canonical pro-cyclist rice-based breakfast/pocket food — easy on the stomach, portable, savoury alternative to sweet oats

- context: everyday, rest-day

- why: balanced carb/protein everyday breakfast, low-fat, easy to batch the soup and rice
