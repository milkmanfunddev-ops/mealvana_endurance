### Overnight oats with soy milk, chia & berries
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: everyday, pre-session
- ingredients: rolled oats 80g, soy milk 250ml, chia seeds 1 tbsp, mixed berries 100g, maple syrup 1 tbsp
- diets_ok: vegan, vegetarian, mediterranean
- allergens: soy, gluten (oats)
- swaps: soy milk→oat or rice milk (soy-free); oats→certified GF oats (gluten-free)
- approx_macros: ~480 kcal, 80g carbs, 16g protein, 10g fat
- prep: 5 min, no-cook (prep night before)
- source: staple across vegan endurance meal-prep guides (No Meat Athlete, r/veganfitness); commonly reported format
- why: high-carb, portable, familiar pre-session breakfast; soy milk adds protein oats alone lack
