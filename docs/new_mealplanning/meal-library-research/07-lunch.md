### Chicken, rice & broccoli meal-prep bowl with teriyaki
- meal_type: lunch
- context: everyday
- ingredients: chicken breast 150g (grilled/baked), jasmine or brown rice 200g cooked, broccoli 150g steamed, teriyaki sauce 2 tbsp, sesame seeds 1 tsp
- diets_ok: omnivore, pescatarian (swap chicken), low_carb (reduce rice)
- allergens: soy, sesame
- swaps: teriyaki (tamari-based, check label) → coconut aminos removes soy/gluten risk; omit sesame seeds for sesame-free
- approx_macros: ~620 kcal, 70g carb, 45g protein, 12g fat
- prep: batch — cook Sunday, 4-5 servings, reheats well
- source: staple "meal-prep bowl" format across triathlete meal-prep guides, e.g. The Kitchn "A Triathlete's Sunday Meal Prep Routine" (https://www.thekitchn.com/a-triathletes-sunday-meal-prep-routine-235207) and Triathlete.com meal-prep guide (https://www.triathlete.com/nutrition/the-busy-triathletes-guide-to-meal-prepping/)
- why: the canonical component-first triathlete lunch — protein+grain+veg+sauce, batch-cooked once for the week

### Quinoa, black bean, beet-green & feta salad (Molly Huddle style)
- meal_type: lunch
- context: everyday, recovery
- ingredients: quinoa 1 cup cooked, black beans 1 cup, beet greens (or spinach) 3 cups wilted, garlic 1 clove, red pepper 1/2, cashews 1/4 cup, feta 1/2 cup, scallions/cilantro, chili-lime seasoning

- prep: batch — cook once, 4-6 servings, keeps 4-5 days
- source: Olympian Molly Huddle's own recipe, made as dinner then eaten as next-day recovery lunch — ESPN "What Athletes Eat" (https://www.espn.com/espnw/life-style/story/_/id/23092614/what-athletes-eat-boston-marathoner-molly-huddle-quinoa-salad-quick-recovery)
