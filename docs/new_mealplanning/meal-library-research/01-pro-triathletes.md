Note: most pro-triathlete media coverage is race-day/fuelling focused, not everyday-diet focused. Attributions are as reported by the source; several are aggregated round-ups (e.g. active.com) rather than long-form profiles.

### Almond butter honey toast with hemp seeds & OJ
- meal_type: breakfast
- context: everyday
- ingredients: Dave's Killer Bread (or similar whole-grain seeded bread) 2 slices, almond butter 2 tbsp, honey 1 tbsp, hemp seeds 2 tbsp, orange juice 1 glass, cappuccino with 2% milk
- diets_ok: vegetarian, mediterranean
- allergens: gluten, tree_nuts, dairy
- swaps: bread→certified GF bread (gluten-free); almond butter→sunflower seed butter (tree-nut-free); milk→oat milk (dairy-free, vegan)
- approx_macros: ~550 kcal, ~75g carbs, ~15g protein, ~20g fat
- prep: 5 min
- source: Paula Findlay's 7am breakfast, reported in "What Olympian and 70.3 Champion Paula Findlay eats in a day," triathlonmagazine.ca
- why: fast, low-fibre, familiar carb-forward breakfast that fits before a first training session
