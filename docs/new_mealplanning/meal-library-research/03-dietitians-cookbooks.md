### Allen Lim's Rice Cakes (savory bacon-egg rice cake)
- meal_type: snack
- context: pre-session, everyday
- ingredients: cooked Calrose/sticky rice 2 cups (dry), bacon 8oz cooked & chopped, eggs 4 scrambled, low-sodium soy sauce or liquid aminos 2 tbsp, brown sugar to taste, salt, grated parmesan (optional)
- diets_ok: omnivore
- allergens: eggs, soy
- swaps: bacon → sautéed mushrooms/tempeh bacon (vegetarian); soy sauce → tamari (still soy) or coconut aminos (soy-free); omit parmesan (dairy-free)
- approx_macros: ~350 kcal, ~55g carbs, ~12g protein, ~9g fat per cake (yields ~6-8 cakes from batch)
- prep: batch — cook rice, mix, press into pan, cut into squares; wrap in foil; do not freeze (texture ruins)
- source: Allen Lim & Biju Thomas, Feed Zone Cookbook/Feed Zone Portables — the signature recipe of the book, used by pro cyclists (Radioshack/Garmin-era pros); https://feedzonecookbook.com/, http://www.bicyclefriends.com/2013/07/the-feed-zone-allen-lims-rice-cake.html
- why: THE canonical on-bike/on-run real-food alternative to gels; sticky rice base transports and digests well during long sessions
