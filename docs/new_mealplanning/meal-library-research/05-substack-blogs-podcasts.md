### Build-a-Bowl with lemon dijon vinaigrette (whole grain, protein, veg)
- meal_type: lunch
- context: everyday, rest-day
- ingredients: cooked whole grain (rice/quinoa/farro) 1.5 cups dry per batch, protein (chicken/salmon/tofu) 1.5 lb per batch, mixed vegetables 4-5 cups, avocado oil, lemon dijon vinaigrette (lemon juice, apple cider vinegar, olive oil, dijon mustard, garlic, honey) 2 tbsp per serving
- diets_ok: omnivore, pescatarian, vegetarian (with tofu), mediterranean, low_carb (reduce grain)
- allergens: none as listed (check mustard/soy if tofu used)
- swaps: chicken→tofu or chickpeas (vegetarian/vegan); grain portion down (lower-carb)
- approx_macros: ~620 kcal, 70g carbs, 44g protein, 20g fat per serving
- prep: batch — build components Sunday, assemble bowls all week
- source: Featherstone Nutrition (RD-run recipe blog), featherstonenutrition.com/build-a-bowl-lemon-dijon-vinaigrette/
- why: canonical "component-first" batch-cook template athletes rotate through all week — exactly the chicken+rice+veg+sauce pattern our research flags as the staple rotation.
