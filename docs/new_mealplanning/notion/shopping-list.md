## Description

Athletes and coaches spend significant time figuring out what products to buy and where to get them. Once a fueling plan is generated — whether for training weeks or race day — the app should be able to compile all recommended foods and sports nutrition products into a consolidated, actionable shopping list.

This feature aggregates nutrition products across an athlete's upcoming plans and presents them in a shareable, checkable list. It should integrate with [theFeed.com](http://theFeed.com)'s product catalog (already populated in our Supabase database) to link directly to purchasable items, and eventually support general grocery items for meal planning.

**Key considerations:**
- Auto-generate from planned nutrition across a configurable time window (e.g., this week's training block)
- Group items by category (sports nutrition, groceries, supplements)
- Show quantities needed based on planned consumption
- Link to purchase options (theFeed.com integration, affiliate links)
- Support coach brand preferences — if a coach has a partnership with a specific brand, prioritize those products
- Shareable format so athletes can hand it to someone else or use it at the store

**Related problems from coach interviews:**
- Cost influences product choices but is not represented in planning
- Athletes can't translate gram targets into feasible product mixes — a shopping list closes the loop from plan to procurement
