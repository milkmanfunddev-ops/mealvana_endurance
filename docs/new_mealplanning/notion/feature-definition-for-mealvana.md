## Introduction

### Value Proposal or General Description
Mealvana is a one-stop shop for all your meal planning needs. From the grocery store to the kitchen, Mealvana is there to help you answer the redundant question: "What's for dinner?" All from your phone.

### Scenario description
We provide several (5+) scenarios to help you enjoy preparing your dishes. (Here we introduce the scenarios that users may come across while working on related problems)

---

**Customizing Meal Plans for Your Week**
Imagine you're prepping for a busy week ahead and want a tailored meal plan that fits your needs. Mealvana makes this process seamless, starting from planning to shopping for ingredients and finally cooking.

---

### Scenario 1: Designing Next Week's Meal Plan
**User Process:**
It's Saturday morning, and you're sipping coffee while thinking about what you and your family will eat next week. You pull out your notebook or open a note-taking app, trying to recall favorite meals or looking up new recipes online. You might be juggling different goals—keeping meals balanced, ensuring variety, catering to dietary restrictions, or even just trying to avoid food waste.

**Pain Points:**
- It's overwhelming to balance variety, nutrition, and preferences.
- The process feels time-consuming.
- You have to cross-reference multiple resources (pantry, recipes, and sales).

**Solutions:**
- See My Plan Recommendation
- Automized shopping list
- Include Ingredients Feature

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
With Mealvana, you can open the app and start by inputting how many breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and snacks you need for the week. Based on your preferences, the app suggests recipes that meet your dietary and time constraints. You can easily customize your plan, review it, and adjust serving sizes—all without leaving the app. These meal plans are generated at the tap of a button taking away the painstakingly long process of searching for a recipe. In addition, Mealvana can help you switch it up. The recommendations in the "See My Plan" function help the user get a diversified meal plan every time they use the app. If you hate mushrooms or are allergic, you can change.

---

### Scenario 2: Shopping for the Meal Plan
**User Process:**
Your meal plan is complete, and it's time to head to the grocery store. You pull up your handwritten list, but it's disorganized, and you worry that you might forget something. You try to check store sales online or flip through flyers for deals, which adds more time to the process.

**Pain Points:**
- The list is disorganized, and you waste time retracing steps in the store.
- It's hard to keep track of store sales without jumping between different apps.
- You forget items or end up with unnecessary impulse buys.

**Solutions:**
- Categorized shopping list
- Browse Coupons with embedded Include Ingredients Feature
- Complete shopping lists that are automatically generated

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
Mealvana auto-generates a shopping list based on your meal plan, so no more missing ingredients! Mealvana also organizes items by store departments, making it easier to navigate the aisles without running in circles through the store. This organized list can help reduce random impulse buys because you are a Man with a Plan and know exactly all the items you need. Plus, by entering your zip code, you can browse local deals and coupons directly within the app, saving you time and money. In addition, if you see an item on sale, you can directly include it in the ingredients you want to have in your weekly meal plan. You will go into the store knowing you are getting a good deal, rather than having to plan on the fly a meal that supports whatever is currently on sale. If you're shopping online, the app even connects with local stores for delivery or curbside pickup options. Mealvana helps take the guesswork and time waste out of shopping lists.

---

### Scenario 3: Cooking the Meals
**User Process:**
It's Tuesday evening, and after a long day, you start preparing dinner. You pull up the recipe, but halfway through, you realize you're missing an ingredient. Or maybe you're unsure about cooking times, so you have to search for a timer. The process is a little more stressful than you hoped.

**Pain Points:**
- Realizing you're missing ingredients mid-cooking.
- Having to toggle between your phone and the stove for instructions.
- Losing track of cooking times.

**Solutions:**
- Complete shopping lists that are automatically generated
- A handsfree app with a cooking mode (always on feature)
- Built-in, stackable timers

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
When it's time to cook, Mealvana's cooking mode walks you through the steps, complete with built-in timers. The timers stack, so you can have multiple going at the same time, or if that chicken needs a little longer, you can add time (in 30 second intervals) to your timer. With the optimization of the cooking process, Mealvana ensures that all the prep work actually occurs prior to needing the ingredient. Say goodbye to quickly rough cutting vegetables you didn't know you needed. Also, the hands-free navigation lets you wave through recipe steps without touching your phone. Recipes also have built in tips that can help you learn to cook like a pro. The app also reminds you about ingredient substitutions if you're missing something, keeping the cooking process smooth and stress-free.

### Scenario 4: Browsing Recipes for Fun
**User Process:**
You're lounging on the couch, scrolling through social media, and you feel like exploring some new meal ideas. You want to find inspiration, maybe something you haven't tried before, or see what's trending in different cuisines.

**Pain Points:**
- Sifting through random recipe sites or blogs for something new.
- Difficulty finding specific types of recipes (e.g., quick meals, vegan).
- Inability to save or categorize interesting recipes easily.

**Solutions:**
- Recipes Page
- Categories and onboarding diet restrictions
- Favorite Recipes list

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
Mealvana's recipe browsing feature lets you casually scroll through curated recipes by category, with filters for dietary preferences, time constraints, or trending dishes. And, added bonus, they are all in the same place! You can preview full recipes, view ingredients, and adjust serving sizes all from one screen, saving your favorites to try later. The preview can save you from adding a recipe to your meal plan to realize later it has a very important ingredient you aren't particularly fond of. Then, later when you want to build your meal plan, Mealvana will include your favorites into your meal plans (with a little variety).

---

### Scenario 5: Using the Diary Feature
**User Process:**
It's Monday evening, and after dinner, you want to review your meals for the next day. You remember you planned a lunch you weren't too sure about, so you want to make sure it still fits with your overall goals for the week.

**Pain Points:**
- Difficulty tracking whether meals align with specific goals (nutrition, portion sizes).
- Forgetting what meals are planned for upcoming days.

**Solutions:**
- Diary Feature
- Past Meal Plans and Meal Plans page

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
Mealvana's diary feature lets you look at your meal plan day by day, reviewing nutritional details and portion sizes for each meal. With the diary tracking feature, you can look at the cumulative calculation of your daily macro-intake based on your Mealvana meals. This says you from having to individual look up and calculate the nutrition information of each ingredient. It also lets you log how well each meal worked for you, so over time, the app can better tailor its recommendations based on your real-life experience. The meal plan saves you past meals plans too, so if you make a couple different meal plans, you can keep track of what is next on the menu!

### Scenario 6: Finding a Cost-Effective Meal Plan
**User Process:**
You're on a tight budget this week and want to make sure you plan meals that maximize savings without sacrificing variety or nutrition. Normally, this involves a lot of research into grocery store sales and juggling meal choices to use cheaper ingredients.

**Pain Points:**
- Having to manually track coupons and sales across different stores.
- Difficulty creating a meal plan that balances savings with variety.

**Solutions:**
- Zip Code based coupons for different stores
- Include ingredients feature

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
With Mealvana, you can enter your budget and zip code, and the app will suggest a cost-effective meal plan that includes discounted ingredients based on local deals. Lose the paper trail of coupons following you around the store. With Mealvana, you'll know what's on sale before you even enter the store! You can tweak the plan to add variety or adjust for specific preferences without losing sight of your budget. You can also get several different flavor profile recipes with the include ingredients function that include similar ingredients to maximize savings, minimize waste, and ensure you are still getting variety.

### Scenario 7: Planning with a Purpose
**User Process:**
You're planning for the upcoming week but have a specific goal in mind—whether it's staying fit, preparing quick meals, or creating something special for a holiday. Balancing your goal with everyday meals can feel daunting.

**Pain Points:**
- Difficulty finding recipes that align with specific goals (e.g., fitness, health).
- Struggling to plan around special occasions without disrupting the regular meal schedule.

**Solutions:**
- Pre and post workout recipes and Nutrition feature
- Special Occasion feature like: Date Night, Gatherings, and Thanksgiving

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
Whether you're planning for a fitness routine, want healthier meals, or need a delicious spread for an upcoming holiday, Mealvana tailors meal plans to your goals. The app suggests recipes that match your focus and helps you manage both day-to-day meals and special occasions without stress. The app has recipes that are health-oriented, quick and easy, and even pre and post workout ingredient to maximize your food fueling. In addition, you can create special meal plans for events, date nights, or even holidays. The app will keep a backlog of your previous meal plans, so you can multitask your planning and even have two separate grocery lists!

### Minor Scenario 1: Customizing Your Own Recipes
**User Process:**
You love trying new recipes but often find yourself modifying them to suit your tastes or dietary restrictions. While this works well, it's frustrating that you have to manually track the changes every time.

**Pain Points:**
- Constantly re-customizing recipes from scratch.
- Having to manually calculate nutrition after making changes.

**Solutions:**
- Edit and save Meal Card
- Automated Nutrition calculation

**How Mealvana Can Help:**
With Mealvana, you can customize and save your favorite meals directly in the app, keeping track of adjustments and instantly updating the nutritional information to reflect your changes. For example, tired of broccoli, rice, protein? Swap out the broccoli for brussel sprouts, rice for quinoa, or add a sauce to mix it up. Then you can save this meal for future use! You can edit all of this from the Review Meal Card page.

### Meal Planning
Fulfill all your meal planning needs. This page walks you from the planning stage to the cooking stage while allowing for complete customization every step of the way!

**Functionalities:**
- **Create new meal plan**
  - indicate how many different breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack recipes you want, respectively
  - indicate the serving size
  - include ingredients: type in ingredients you want to use and you will get a meal plan with a recipe using that ingredient
  - browse coupons: enter your local zip code (using flipp technology) to browse local grocers' coupons
  - can add ingredients to shopping list directly from the coupon page
- **See my plan**
  - A meal plan is created with respect to all the previous specifications (number of meals, serving size, and ingredients)
  - Can add to your meal plan in each meal category (breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack)
  - Can customize your own meal card and that meal will get saved
  - Can filter the meals
  - Can search with keywords through the meals
  - Click on the meals to view and edit the meal card
    - can browse meal's nutrition
    - can delete recipes you want to disinclude from the meal
    - can add recipes: get taken to the recipes page with an automatic meal category filter applied (for example if you are editing a lunch meal then an automatic lunch filter is applied)
    - can change serving size
  - Confirm plan
- **Build it myself**
  - Can add to your meal plan in each meal category (breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack)
  - Can customize your own meal card and that meal will get saved
  - Can filter the meals
  - Can search with keywords through the meals
  - Click on the meals to view and edit the meal card
    - can browse meal's nutrition
    - can delete recipes you want to disinclude from the meal
    - can add recipes: get taken to the recipes page with an automatic meal category filter applied
    - can change serving size
  - Confirm plan
- **Edit meal plan and Past Meal Plans**
  - can make changes to the meal plan or meals
  - can view past meal plans
  - can copy past meal plans and make edits to it before confirming
- **Diary Feature**
  - can add meals from current meal plan to diary
  - can change to a past meal plan and add meals to diary
  - can view old diary entries
- **Messages**
  - Can click the bell to view messages from Mealvana
- **Purchase Cookies**
  - can view the breakdown of cookies to see counts of bonus, earned, spent, and current cookies
  - can purchase 5 cookies for $2.99
- **Previewing Recipes:**
  - View detailed ingredients and step-by-step instructions.
  - Adjust serving sizes to automatically update ingredient quantities.
  - Rate, leave feedback, and browse nutritional information.
  - Identify recipes with icons indicating gluten-free, vegetarian, or vegan options.
  - Share recipes or start cooking directly from the preview page.
- **Cooking Mode:**
  - Swipe or hover your hand to navigate recipe steps.
  - Adjust servings to dynamically update ingredient quantities in the instructions.
  - Use interactive features like tips (marked by a lit lightbulb before use) and built-in timers:
    - Timers are embedded in steps and can be adjusted in 30-second increments.
    - Multiple timers can run simultaneously, with options to pause or resume.
    - Notifications and sound alerts ensure you never miss a timer.
  - Jump to any step at any time for flexibility while cooking.
  - After completing the recipe, users are prompted to rate and provide feedback.

### Shopping
Enjoy an automatically generated shopping list with all the ingredients for your meal plan—organized and categorized so you can shop efficiently!

**Functionalities:**
- **Shopping Item List**
  - Mark each item as checked when it's purchased.
  - Delete any item, with a brief window to undo the deletion.
  - Click on any item to view:
    - Substitutions and images for hard-to-identify ingredients.
    - The recipe(s) that the ingredient belongs to.
  - Add custom items:
    - If the item is in our predefined set of ingredients, it is automatically categorized.
    - If not, you can manually assign a category from our predefined list.
    - You can also access flyers and add items while browsing them.
  - Add items to a "Frequently Bought" list so they persist even after logging out or uninstalling the app.
  - Automatically collapse categories that are fully checked off.
- **Shopping Online**
  - For example, if your shopping list reads "2 slices of whole grain bread," only "whole grain bread" is sent to the vendor's website.
  - Custom-added items are also sent to vendor websites.
- **Share Shopping List**
  Easily share your shopping list with others through text.
- **Access Past Shopping Carts**
  Navigate forward, backward, or use a calendar view to review your shopping history.
