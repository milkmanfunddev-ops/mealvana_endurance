## Meal Planning Feature Discussion

**Strategic Approach:**

The team had extensive discussion about meal planning as a major new feature that could differentiate the product.

**Design Philosophy:**

- Focus on minimal user effort — closer to Hungry Root's approach than complex recipe planning
- Emphasize batch cooking with pre-prepped ingredients that can be easily combined
- Use partially prepped foods (sous vide chicken, ready rice) to reduce cooking time
- Target 1-2 planning sessions per week with shopping lists generated
- Design should solve the "what's for dinner" problem efficiently

**Initial Scope:**

- Start with single athletes without families to simplify initial design
- Design around athlete's training needs with nutrition periodization
- Users with families can adapt meal plans themselves (e.g., scaling recipes)
- App will understand training context and make intelligent recommendations
