## Description

Athletes and coaches see numbers — 65g carbs/hr, 1,840 kcal TDEE, 142g protein — but have no idea where those numbers come from. When a recommendation feels off ("Why does it say 80g carbs for this easy run?"), there's no way to inspect the logic. This erodes trust, leads athletes to override without understanding, and generates unnecessary back-and-forth between coaches and athletes.

Nutrition Transparency adds an explainability layer across the app. Anywhere the algorithm produces a number — workout macro targets, daily macro calculations, food recommendations — a (?) icon opens a clear, human-readable breakdown of exactly how that number was calculated, what inputs drove it, and what assumptions were made.

This directly addresses two validated needs: the "show the why behind recommendations" idea from the Critical Usability Issues list, and the trust problem identified in coach interviews where Claudia closed the app after seeing recommendations that felt "so off" — if she could have seen *why* the numbers were what they were, she might have adjusted inputs rather than abandoning the tool.
