### Show the prototype (10 mins)
1. Looking at this race-day tool, what is the first piece of information your eyes go to? Why does that matter most to you as a coach?
2. Does this layout help you make a faster decision about an athlete's plan, or does it add cognitive load?
3. We want to ensure this tool is easy to use but effective. What is currently missing that would make you feel more confident in the plan you're giving your athlete?

### Daily Training Nutrition (5 mins)
1. How do you currently ensure the athlete practice race day nutrition? Is that something important to you? How do you currently verify that an athlete is actually "practicing" their fueling during training?
2. On a scale of 1–5, how often do race day failures come from a lack of daily practice (gut training) versus a poor race day plan itself? Why that number?
3. If a tool could bridge the gap between your plan and their daily compliance, how would that change your coaching efficacy on a scale of 1–5?
4. Where in the daily tracking process do you feel the most friction? Is it the data entry for the athlete, or the data synthesis for you?

### Show the coach mode (5 mins)
1. Looking at this dashboard, does it provide the "at-a-glance" clarity you need to manage your athletes, or is it too granular?
2. Beyond just the numbers, what else would you need to see here to feel that you are meaningfully serving your athletes' long-term well-being?
3. If you could change one thing about how you and your athletes talk about data, what would it be?
