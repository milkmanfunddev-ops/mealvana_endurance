## Script

1. "I'm very curious about your process in planning meals. Walk me through very quickly the last time you did grocery shopping and how did you decide what to shop for."
   1. Tell me how you decide for dinner today.
   2. (share screen, show two images) Is your meal more like ingredients put/resembled together, or is your meal more driven by recipe?
2. "There are no right answers; honest reactions are what I need, including 'this is confusing.'"
   1. This is only a prototype, not all buttons work; pre-saved recipe has limits and may not tailor to personal preference.
   2. Used a randomized persona and sample data for demonstration purposes.
   3. Informed by training plan, has memory of preference.
3. Task: "Create a mealplan and a shopping list"
   1. Click through prototype 1 — role-play as "Caroline," a female athlete with a partner, using training data and food preference. "What would you do to complete your next week's meal plan?" "How would you describe this?"
   2. Click through prototype 2 — "How would you describe this?"
   3. "Which word would you apply to the design?"
4. On a scale of 1–5 (1 boring, 5 engaging): overall design, easiness, personalized.

### Optional probes
- Did either prototype match how you think of meal planning? (traditional structured interface vs. chat-bot AI; user wants more control vs. user needs more direction)
- "In one of these, the system led with suggestions. In the other, you led by telling it what you wanted. Which felt right to you?"
- Forced choice: "If you had to pick one to use weekly for the next month — which? Why?"

## Two prototype differences (working notes)
- Traditional structured interface vs. chat-bot AI (leading AI chat)
- Assumptions: user wants more control vs. user needs more direction
- Just ingredients vs. detailed recipe
