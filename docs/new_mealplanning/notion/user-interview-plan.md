We're developing a training-aware meal and nutrition planning app for endurance athletes. Early discovery with this population shows strong demand for consistent, performance-focused planning, but we still lack a clear picture of athletes' meal planning processes, where current tools break down, and what value they would actually pay for.

## Research Goal
This user interview research aims to deepen our understanding of endurance athletes' core challenges and decision criteria, so we can validate or overturn key assumptions, surface unmet needs across segments, and prioritize our product features.

## Core Research Questions
- *(highlighted in the source doc)* What is the trade-off between convenience and personalization accuracy?
- *(highlighted)* What motivates athletes to keep using a nutrition app every week, and what causes them to lapse or churn?
- *(highlighted)* Where do existing tools, spreadsheets, or "wing-it" methods break down for them?
- How will athletes know the app is "working" for them (subjective feelings, performance metrics, lab results)?
- Which endurance-athlete segments feel the greatest pain around nutrition planning and are most willing to pay?
- What concerns (data accuracy, allergy handling, privacy, coach disagreement) could block adoption or payment?
- What are female athletes' unique needs in nutrition planning?

## Research Methods
- **Format**: One-on-one virtual user interview sessions
- **Tools**: Zoom, Dovetail
- **Participants**: Endurance athletes
- **Structure**: 30 minutes with a script
