## Content

### "I'm Not a Dietitian" — Scope-of-practice anxiety as the primary barrier to nutrition coaching
**Alex Morrow:** Explicitly limits himself because of scope of practice: *"I am a running coach, I'm not a dietitian, I'm not a nutritionist, so we try to speak anecdotally."* [↗ alexmorrow](https://app.notion.com/p/337e3fdb754c8085947fd8578da79c13#5425c0974199488bab4b188d9b019237) References state licensure variability and personal liability — if he starts designing meal plans and something goes wrong, he's legally exposed. As one of 8 RRCA coaching instructors in the country, he teaches new coaches this same principle: stay in your lane. This is the exact gap Mealvana Endurance fills — giving coaches a tool backed by science and research so the coach doesn't have to be the nutrition expert.

---

### The manufacturer's label as a proxy for expertise — outsourcing nutrition authority to product packaging
**Alex Morrow:** Because he won't prescribe specific nutrition, he defers to manufacturer recommendations as a stand-in for expertise: *"I'm not saying take it every 45 minutes, the manufacturer is saying take it every 45 minutes."* [↗ alexmorrow](https://app.notion.com/p/337e3fdb754c8085947fd8578da79c13#df3e5c628144482885ad529935d48322) He uses the gel package label as the "expert" voice. This workaround reveals how desperately coaches need a credible intermediary — they want to give nutrition guidance but need something authoritative between them and the athlete.

---

### Coaches want correlation tools, not prescription tools — "I don't want to be the person that manipulates that"
**Alex Morrow:** Was explicit in his demo feedback: *"I don't even need to change it. I don't want to be the person that manipulates that because I'm not an expert in that area."* What he wants is data correlation — seeing that an athlete had three low-carb days aligned with three days of missed workout paces. He needs the overview, not the build. His ideal workflow: check what an athlete ate when performance was good (make note) or bad (investigate). This confirms the coach dashboard should default to read-only summary views with nutrition-performance correlation, not plan-building tools.
