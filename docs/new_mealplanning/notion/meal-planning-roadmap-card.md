## Description

While Mealvana Endurance's core strength is workout-specific fueling, many athletes and coaches want help with the rest of the plate — the everyday meals that support training adaptation, recovery, and body composition goals. Today, athletes either wing it, hire a separate dietitian, or use generic meal planning apps that don't account for training load.

This feature lets athletes plan simple, nutritious weekly meals that take their training and recovery needs into account. The system generates meal suggestions calibrated to the athlete's daily macro targets (from the daily macro calculation engine), training schedule, dietary preferences, and time constraints. Shopping lists are generated automatically.

### Key considerations
- Meals should be practical and time-conscious — endurance athletes are often time-crunched; focus on simple, repeatable meals rather than gourmet recipes
- Integration with daily macro calculation: meals should fill the nutritional gaps left after workout-specific fueling is accounted for
- Support dietary preferences and restrictions (vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, etc.)
- Auto-generated shopping lists (connects to the Shopping List feature)
- Training-aware: heavier training days get higher-calorie meal plans; rest days get recovery-focused meals
- Coach visibility: coaches can review and suggest modifications to meal plans
- This is a "Cookies" tier feature — premium offering that differentiates from free workout fueling
- Long-term: consider HelloFresh-style integration for automatic ordering (suggested by coach Ryan Bolton)

### Related problems from coach interviews
- Athletes can't translate gram targets into feasible product mixes — extends beyond sports nutrition to everyday food
- Evolving nutrition science creates a perpetual re-education burden — automated meal planning removes the guesswork from daily eating
