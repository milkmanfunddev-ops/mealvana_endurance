# Additional Factors to Consider for Future Meal Planning:

The following is coming from [a ChatGPT conversation](https://chatgpt.com/share/67eeb553-93d8-800d-a8c0-f7d01702f9d5) —

Here's a **thorough breakdown** of the types of information that would help create a highly tailored meal plan. These fall into several categories: **biological, behavioral, environmental, and goal-oriented** data.

---

## 🧠 1. Biological & Health Data

### 🔹 Basic Demographics

- **Age**
- **Sex**
- **Height**
- **Weight**
- **Ethnicity (optional)** – for cultural relevance and metabolic tendencies

### 🔹 Health Status

- **Medical conditions** (e.g., diabetes, PCOS, IBS, thyroid issues)
- **Allergies** (e.g., nuts, shellfish, gluten)
- **Food intolerances** (e.g., lactose, FODMAPs)
- **Digestive issues** (e.g., GERD, constipation)
- **Current medications or supplements**
- **Iron/B12/Vitamin D status** (especially for women, athletes, or vegetarians)
- **Pregnant/Breastfeeding?**

### 🔹 Baseline Biometrics (if available)

- **Body composition** (fat %, lean mass)
- **Metabolic rate (BMR)** or VO2 max
- **Blood sugar, cholesterol, BP levels**

---

## 🏃‍♀️ 2. Lifestyle & Activity Level

### 🔹 Activity Type

- **Daily activity level** (sedentary, light, moderate, intense)
- **Workout types & frequency** (strength training, endurance, HIIT, yoga, etc.)
- **Training goal** (e.g., race training, muscle gain, fat loss)
- **Training duration & timing** (especially important for pre/post nutrition)

### 🔹 Sleep & Stress

- **Hours of sleep/night**
- **Sleep quality**
- **Stress level** (chronic stress impacts metabolism and cravings)

---

## 🍽️ 3. Dietary Preferences & Behaviors

### 🔹 Food Preferences

- **Preferred cuisines or flavors**
- **Diet type** (e.g., vegan, paleo, Mediterranean, DASH)
- **Religious or cultural restrictions** (e.g., halal, kosher)

### 🔹 Eating Patterns

- **How many meals per day?**
- **Intermittent fasting?**
- **Snacking habits?**
- **Typical mealtimes**

### 🔹 Cooking Habits

- **Cooking skill level**
- **Time available to cook per meal**
- **Access to kitchen appliances** (oven, stove, microwave, blender, etc.)
- **Willingness to meal prep or batch cook**
- **Do they like leftovers?**

### 🔹 Food Tracking Comfort

- Willing to count calories/macros?
- Prefer intuitive eating or simple portion guidance?

---

## 🌍 4. Environmental & Social Context

### 🔹 Grocery Access

- **Access to grocery stores/farmers markets**
- **Budget for food per week**
- **Do they shop online or in person?**
- **Availability of certain ingredients in their region**

### 🔹 Household

- **Number of people in household**
- **Do they eat alone or with others?**
- **Any picky eaters or kids?**
- **Shared meals or individual meals?**

### 🔹 Work & Lifestyle

- **Work schedule** (night shifts, travel, work-from-home)
- **Time constraints during the week**
- **Do they eat at work/school/campus often?**
- **Do they rely on restaurants/takeout?**

---

## 🎯 5. Goals & Preferences

### 🔹 Health/Fitness Goals

- **Primary goal:** (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, manage blood sugar, run a 10K)
- **Secondary goal:** (e.g., improve energy, sleep better, reduce cravings)
- **Target weight or fitness metric (if applicable)**

### 🔹 Nutritional Targets

- **Calorie range**
- **Macro breakdown** (if known: carbs, protein, fat)
- **Fiber, iron, or micronutrient focus?**
- **Carb timing (e.g., carb-load before workouts)**

### 🔹 Psychological Factors

- **Motivation style** (likes structure vs. flexibility)
- **History of dieting**
- **Relationship with food** (e.g., disordered eating patterns)
- **Level of nutrition knowledge**

---

## 🧾 6. Tracking & Feedback Preferences

- Do they want:
  - A dynamic plan that adjusts weekly?
  - A static 7-day plan repeated over time?
  - Feedback on their food logs?
  - Integration with wearables or apps (Apple Watch, MyFitnessPal)?
