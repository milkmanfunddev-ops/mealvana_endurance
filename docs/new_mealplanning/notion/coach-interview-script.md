## Script

…. your perspective is invaluable to us. Not just as a highly certified coach, but because of your lived experience running a successful coaching business, and going through the grind yourself as a Ultra Runner. I think You understand the **real-world bandwidth and knowledge gaps** that exist for coaches and athletes.

**Our main goal for this quick 30 minutes is simple:** We're exploring the idea of building a **Coach Portal,** a feature that would allow you to see how your athletes are fueling with Mealvana. We want to understand: **Is there a true need for coaches to monitor nutrition and fueling details, and if so, how much time and energy should we devote to building that capability?** We're here to listen to your pain points.

---

1. In your experience, what is the **biggest gap** between the training plans you set for an athlete and their **actual race-day performance**?
   Where does nutrition, including how they eat, *what* they eat, usually fall on the spectrum of athlete issues you address,
   What's your typical approach when an athlete comes to you with GI issues or fueling fatigue?

### Activity 1
Rank the following coaching elements by how much of your time/energy they typically require

**A.** Designing and delivering customized daily training sessions (pace, volume, recovery)
**B.** Managing athlete communication and motivation
**C.** Addressing physical issues (injury prevention, strength/mobility work)
**D. Designing/Monitoring customized race and training nutrition/fueling plans**
**E.** Handling administrative tasks (scheduling, billing, data entry)

Follow up: When you look at the lower-ranked items (those that don't get as much time) is that due to a lack of importance, or more because you don't feel you have the **time or the specialized tools** to handle them with the same confidence?

Is there a piece of data, like post-run notes, weight, or even reported energy levels, that you wish you could see **easily and reliably** to inform your coaching adjustments?

### Activity 2
I'm going to present you with a few common athlete scenarios. Can you please rate your **confidence level** in providing a specific, science-backed solution, using a scale of 1 to 5.

A. Athlete needs a pre-race week carb-loading plan mapped to their body weight/distance (e.g., specific grams/kg).
B. Athlete experiences GI distress (stomach cramps/nausea) during long runs.
C. Athlete needs a specific sodium/fluid replacement plan for a hot 70.3 race.
D. Athlete is struggling with consistent post-run recovery fueling.

Follow up: For those lower-confidence areas, like carb-loading specifics or precise sodium planning, would having a tool that **automatically translates targets into actual food menus and portions** allow you to focus more on the training execution, knowing the fueling is professionally managed?
